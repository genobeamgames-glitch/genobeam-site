Genobeam Account Deletion Request

If you wish to terminate your account and delete your associated data from the Genobeam service, please follow the instructions below.

Steps to Request Deletion

  1. Click the 'Request Account Deletion' button below to open the official request form.
  2. Fill out the required information (e.g., your registered email address).
  3. Submit the form. Our team will verify and process your request within 14 business days.

Data Handling Policy

  • Data to be Deleted: Upon request, all personal information including your profile, activity logs, and preferences will be permanently erased from our active databases.
  • Data to be Retained: Certain data (such as transaction records) may be retained for a specific period as required by applicable laws and regulations (e.g., financial reporting or tax purposes).
